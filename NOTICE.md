## AutoJam modifications

AutoJam is a standalone derivative of the Fingersonic FM Studio example. The AutoJam project added the Jam composition engine, AUTO and Loop performance controls, screen layouts, drum handling, audio mixing changes, tests and documentation. Upstream baseline: [Fingersonic developer kit, main2](https://github.com/lastness09/fingersonic-guitar-devkit/tree/b7247eb9a3c027e8250e409d12fc9fe9dc52a235/examples/main2).
